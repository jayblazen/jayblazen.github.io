Bit Byte Defender

How to Play

• Move with WASD or the Arrow Keys

• Use your abilities and weapons automatically as they fire

• Press ESC to pause

• Collect XP orbs to level up

• Choose upgrades to customize your build

• Survive as long as possible against evolving digital threats

Story

Its 2 am and you have to submit your homework but it's full of bugs.

The bugs and hackers want to stop you from getting an A.

Your mission: Debug the system. Destroy the invaders. Delete the bugs.
